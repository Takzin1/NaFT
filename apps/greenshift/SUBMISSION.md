# GreenShift submission draft (P90)
## Short description
A local-first climate action decision lab for illustrative rice-paddy methane mitigation. Compare uncertain net reductions, choose a budget-feasible portfolio, attach evidence metadata, and replay decision reports before considering hypothetical shadow value.
## Demo walkthrough (about 2 minutes)
1. Show the scenario planner and change adoption; explain baseline and additional emissions.
2. Load synthetic evidence and record self-review; change assumptions to demonstrate invalidation.
3. Set a portfolio budget and show the conservative impact frontier and no-action choice.
4. Export and replay a decision report. Modify a calculated value to demonstrate rejection.
5. Explore hypothetical shadow prices; explain the difference from actual credit income.
